A Letter From My Heart

"Even in the quietest moments, my heart still finds its way back to you."

Hello Jackie,

I hope you are doing well.

I write to you to let you know that I miss you—I really miss you so much.

I also wanted to let you know, once again, that I love you, and you mean so much to me.

Over the time, that might have changed from your perspective, but I really do love you, and I wish and hope one day I’ll get to spend the rest of my life with you. You are an amazing woman—strong, and ready to fight for what you want. I admire that about you, and I love that about you.

I hope as days go on we draw closer to each other, as we once were, and maybe even more. Because honestly, I am still wondering where I did wrong to push you away. I am sorry if I did anything wrong.

I am ready to be open to you, as open as I can get, so that you can get to know me inside out. I wish to know you the same way.

I love you Jackline.

I hope one day you can find it in your heart to love me the same way.
